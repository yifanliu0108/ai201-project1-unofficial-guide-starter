=== DSC 102 - Scalable Analytics Notes ===

Why I saved this: DSC 102 is where "my notebook works on the small example" is no longer
the whole story.

Course-planning note: this is a systems/scalable analytics class. The useful background
is programming comfort, data management ideas from DSC 100, and some willingness to
think about time, memory, and workflow tradeoffs.

Student advice: do not treat DSC 102 as another pandas class. Small examples can hide
the performance and organization problems that show up once data or workflow size grows.

My takeaway: avoid stacking DSC 102 with multiple heavy project classes. It is the kind
of class where late starts become frustrating because debugging includes systems issues,
not only syntax mistakes.
