=== DSC 102 - Systems for Scalable Analytics ===

DSC 102 is the class students associate with systems thinking for data science: how data
workflows scale, how tools process larger data, and why performance matters when datasets
outgrow a simple notebook.

Unofficial advice: do not take DSC 102 as if it is just another pandas class. The course
is easier if you are comfortable with programming, data management ideas from DSC 100,
and the habit of tracing where time and memory go. Students who only practiced small
toy examples often need extra time to understand why scalable systems have different
tradeoffs.

Schedule pairing note: avoid stacking DSC 102 with multiple heavy project courses unless
you have a lighter GE or writing class in the same quarter. It rewards consistent work
because systems assignments can become frustrating when left until the deadline.
