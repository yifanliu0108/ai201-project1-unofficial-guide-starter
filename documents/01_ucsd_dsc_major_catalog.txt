=== UCSD Data Science B.S. - Major Structure Notes ===

Why I saved this: this is the official baseline for the major. Student advice is useful,
but I wanted the RAG system to know the actual structure first.

Facts from the catalog:
- The major is 112 units total: 52 lower-division units and 60 upper-division units.
- Lower division includes 28 units of data science, 16 units of math, and 8 units of
  subject-domain courses.
- The lower-division DSC sequence includes COGS 9, DSC 10/R, DSC 20/R, DSC 30/R, DSC
  40A/R, DSC 40B/R, and DSC 80/R.
- Upper division includes DSC 100, DSC 102, DSC 106, a DSC 140A/CSE 150A option, a
  DSC 140B/CSE 151A option, a DSC 148/CSE 158 option, electives, and the DSC 180A-B
  senior project.

My planning takeaway: do not think of the major as "just Python." DSC 10 starts the
workflow, DSC 20 and DSC 30 build the programming/data-structures base, DSC 40A/B give
the theory foundation, and DSC 80 is the bridge into the upper division. A delay in one
of those classes can push back DSC 100, DSC 102, DSC 106, DSC 140A, or capstone timing.
