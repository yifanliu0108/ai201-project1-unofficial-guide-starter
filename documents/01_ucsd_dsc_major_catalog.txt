=== UCSD Data Science B.S. - Major Structure ===

The official catalog says the data science major is selective/capped and has a large
lower-division sequence before students reach the upper-division DSC courses. Students
should expect lower-division work in programming, data structures, theory foundations,
calculus, linear algebra, and subject-domain courses.

Student planning note: do not treat DSC as "just Python." The major builds through a
sequence: DSC 10 introduces the field, DSC 20 and DSC 30 build programming and data
structures, DSC 40A and DSC 40B give math/theory foundations, and DSC 80 becomes the
first big practice-and-application class. Skipping around the sequence usually makes
later upper-division courses harder because the prerequisites are cumulative.

The catalog also warns that students must satisfy prerequisites for all courses. In
practice, this means a four-year or transfer plan should be built backward from DSC 80,
DSC 100, DSC 102, DSC 106, DSC 140A, and the capstone sequence rather than only from GE
requirements.
