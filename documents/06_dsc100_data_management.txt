=== DSC 100 - Introduction to Data Management ===

DSC 100 is the data management course in the major. Students discuss it as a different
kind of class from DSC 80: less about full notebook-style analysis and more about how
data is represented, queried, organized, and processed.

Unofficial advice from enrollment threads: pay attention to prerequisites and department
permission rules. Non-majors may need an EASy request, and students trying to take DSC
100 while finishing DSC 40B or DSC 80 should not assume a prerequisite exception will be
approved. Check the official prerequisite list and ask the department before building a
schedule that depends on an exception.

Planning note: DSC 100 can be useful before systems or database-heavy electives, but it
does not replace the practical cleaning and analysis habits from DSC 80. For many
students, DSC 80 is the course with broader carryover across later DSC classes.
