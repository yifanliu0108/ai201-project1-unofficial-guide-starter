=== DSC 100 - Data Management / Prereq Notes ===

Why I saved this: DSC 100 is partly a course-planning issue, not just a course-content
issue, because students ask whether they can take it while finishing prerequisites.

Course note: DSC 100 is data management. It is more about how data is represented,
queried, organized, and processed than about doing an end-to-end notebook analysis.

Enrollment note: do not build a schedule assuming an EASy request will override a DSC
100 prerequisite. If someone is still finishing DSC 40B or DSC 80, they should check the
official prerequisite list and ask the department before depending on an exception.

My takeaway: DSC 100 is useful before systems/database-heavy electives, but it does not
replace DSC 80's cleaning and analysis habits. Treat DSC 80 and prerequisites as real
gates.
