=== DSC 140A - ML Foundation Notes ===

Why I saved this: this is the class I would want warning notes for before signing up,
because "machine learning" can sound like library calls when the class is more
mathematical than that.

Student prep note: review probability and linear algebra before the quarter starts,
especially if DSC 40A/40B felt shaky. The point is not only calling a model; it is
understanding assumptions, objectives, errors, and probabilities.

Planning note: if the goal is ML, computer vision, NLP, or deep learning later, DSC 140A
is foundation. Do not treat it as a checkbox.

My takeaway: avoid pairing DSC 140A with another mathematically heavy class unless the
rest of the schedule is intentionally light.
