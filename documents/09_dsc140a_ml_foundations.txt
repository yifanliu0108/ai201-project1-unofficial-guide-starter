=== DSC 140A - Probabilistic Modeling and Machine Learning ===

DSC 140A is one of the machine-learning foundation courses in the UCSD data science
major. Students generally expect it to require more math maturity than the applied
notebook courses. Probability, linear algebra, and comfort with model assumptions matter.

Unofficial advice: review probability and linear algebra before the quarter starts,
especially if DSC 40A/40B felt shaky. The hard part is often not calling a library; it is
understanding what the model is assuming, what objective is being optimized, and how to
interpret errors or probabilities.

Planning note: if you want machine learning, computer vision, NLP, or deep learning
electives later, DSC 140A should be treated as foundation rather than a box to check.
Pairing it with another mathematically heavy class can make the quarter rough.
