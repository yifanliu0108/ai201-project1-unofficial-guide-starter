=== UCSD Data Science Four-Year / Transfer Planning Notes ===

Why I saved this: this is the "what I would tell a newer DSC student" page. It combines
the official sequence with the practical scheduling warnings.

Main rule: build the plan around prerequisites. Finish DSC 10, DSC 20, DSC 30, DSC 40A,
DSC 40B, and DSC 80 on time because they unlock the upper-division core. A delay in one
lower-division class can cascade into DSC 100, DSC 102, DSC 106, DSC 140A, or capstone.

Workload rule: do not stack too many heavy programming, theory, or project courses in
one quarter. A balanced quarter might pair one demanding DSC course with a GE, writing
course, or lighter elective. A rough quarter would stack DSC 30, DSC 40B, and another
technical project class with no real debugging time.

Career note: save evidence as you go: notebooks, visualization projects, systems/database
work, and ML projects. If research is the goal, start talking to faculty, tutors, or labs
before senior year instead of waiting for capstone.
