=== UCSD Data Science Four-Year / Transfer Planning Strategy ===

The safest UCSD data science plan is built around prerequisites. Finish DSC 10, DSC 20,
DSC 30, DSC 40A, DSC 40B, and DSC 80 on time because they unlock the upper-division
core. A delay in one lower-division course can cascade into DSC 100, DSC 102, DSC 106,
DSC 140A, or capstone timing.

Workload pairing matters. Avoid taking too many heavy programming, theory, or project
courses in the same quarter. A balanced quarter might pair one demanding DSC course with
a GE, writing course, or lighter elective. A rough quarter would stack DSC 30, DSC 40B,
and another technical project class without enough debugging/study time.

Career planning note: the major gives breadth, but internships and projects reward
evidence. Save strong notebooks, visualization projects, database/systems work, and ML
projects. If you are interested in research, start talking to faculty, tutors, or labs
before senior year rather than waiting for capstone.
