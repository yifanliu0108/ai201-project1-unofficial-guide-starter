=== DSC 40A and DSC 40B - Theory Foundations ===

DSC 40A and DSC 40B are the theory foundation courses for data science. Students usually
describe them as more math/proof oriented than DSC 10 or DSC 20. The useful mindset is
to practice definitions and derivations early instead of waiting until the exam review.

DSC 40A is commonly treated as the bridge from computation into probability, modeling,
and mathematical reasoning. DSC 40B continues that foundation and becomes important for
later courses that ask why an algorithm or model works rather than only how to run it.

Unofficial advice: do not pair DSC 40B with too many other proof-heavy or project-heavy
classes if you can avoid it. Students who are comfortable with calculus and linear
algebra have an easier time, but the course still requires steady problem practice.
