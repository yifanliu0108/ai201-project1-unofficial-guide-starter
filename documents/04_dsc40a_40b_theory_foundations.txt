=== DSC 40A and DSC 40B - Theory Foundation Notes ===

Why I saved this: these classes are easy to schedule as just "another DSC requirement,"
but they are the theory base for the rest of the major.

DSC 40A/B are more math and reasoning heavy than DSC 10 or DSC 20. The useful work is
not only doing homework right before it is due; it is keeping definitions, derivations,
and assumptions straight while the quarter is happening.

Planning note: DSC 40A is the bridge from computation into probability/modeling-style
reasoning. DSC 40B keeps building the foundation that later shows up when a class asks
why a model works, not just how to call a package.

My takeaway: avoid pairing DSC 40B with too many other proof-heavy or project-heavy
classes. If calculus or linear algebra is rusty, give this sequence more space.
