# Improving Steam Power: The Rancilio Silvia Steam Wand Mod

*Source: r/gaggiaclassic + Home-Barista mod guides (paraphrased)*

A frequent complaint about the Gaggia Classic (older models) is that the stock steam wand and its multi-hole "panarello" tip make weak, bubbly froth instead of tight microfoam. Two things are happening: the panarello aerates too aggressively for latte art, and people often steam before the boiler is fully up to steam temperature.

First, the easy fixes that cost nothing:

- After pressing the steam button, **wait for the steam-ready light** before opening the valve. The boiler must climb to steam temperature.
- **Purge the wand first** — open the steam valve briefly to clear condensed water from the line so you steam with dry steam, not spitting water.
- Keep the tip just below the milk surface to introduce air at the start (stretching), then submerge to roll/texture.

The popular hardware upgrade is the **Rancilio Silvia steam wand mod**: you swap the stock Gaggia wand for a Rancilio Silvia articulating wand fitted with a **single-hole steam tip**. The single hole concentrates the steam into a faster, more controllable jet that creates a tight whirlpool and glossy microfoam suitable for latte art. The Silvia wand also articulates (ball joint), making positioning easier. It's a direct-fit popular mod and pairs well with the PID mod.

Note: newer Gaggia Classic Pro models already ship with a commercial-style single-hole wand, so the mod mainly benefits older Classic units with the panarello.
