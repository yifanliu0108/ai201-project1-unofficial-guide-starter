=== Enrollment, Second Pass, and EASy Notes ===

Why I saved this: enrollment advice is the part that official four-year plans do not
fully capture. A perfect plan on paper can fail if one bottleneck class gets delayed.

Student rule of thumb: first pass classes that are bottlenecks, prerequisites for
multiple later courses, or hard to replace. A class that unlocks three later courses is
more urgent than a class that can be taken almost any quarter.

EASy note: do not rely on an EASy request for a prerequisite exception unless an advisor
or department confirms it. Asking is fine; building the whole schedule around approval is
the risky part.

Transfer note: check petitions early. A course from another UC or community college may
look similar to a UCSD requirement, but equivalency is not automatic.
