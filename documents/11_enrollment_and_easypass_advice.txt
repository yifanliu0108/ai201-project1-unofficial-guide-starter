=== Enrollment, Second Pass, and EASy Requests for DSC Classes ===

Student enrollment advice for UCSD DSC classes is practical: know which courses fill,
which ones majors can usually second pass, and which ones require permission if you are
not in the major. Older r/UCSD discussions say DSC majors often have enough priority for
some core DSC classes, but that does not mean every quarter is safe.

Unofficial advice: first pass classes that are bottlenecks for your plan, prerequisites
for multiple later courses, or hard to replace. If a class is required before several
upper divisions, a delay can push back your whole schedule. Do not rely on an EASy request
for a prerequisite exception unless an advisor or department confirms it.

For transfer students, check petitions early. A course from another UC or community
college may look similar to DSC 80 or another UCSD requirement, but equivalency is not
automatic. Ask before enrolling if your plan depends on the course counting.
