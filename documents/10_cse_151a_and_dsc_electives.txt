=== CSE 151A, DSC Electives, and Technical Direction Notes ===

Why I saved this: the DSC major is broad enough that two students can graduate with very
different strengths. Elective choices matter.

Student discussion theme: CSE 151A is often mentioned as a strong ML option when it is
available, while DSC electives vary by topic and instructor. Seat availability and
prerequisites can make "I'll just take the ML elective later" less reliable than it
sounds.

Planning note: the major alone does not automatically create a deep ML portfolio. The
portfolio comes from projects, research, internships, or a coherent elective path in ML,
databases, systems, visualization, or a domain application.

My takeaway: pick a direction early enough that upper-division electives reinforce each
other instead of feeling like random requirement-filling.
