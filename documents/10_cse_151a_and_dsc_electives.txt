=== CSE 151A, DSC Electives, and Choosing a Technical Direction ===

UCSD data science students often compare DSC machine-learning classes with CSE electives.
Student discussions say the major can feel broad, so your elective choices matter a lot.
If you want a more technical path, choose electives that build depth instead of only
checking easy requirements.

Unofficial advice: CSE 151A is commonly viewed as a strong machine-learning option when
available to you, while DSC electives vary by topic and instructor. Students interested
in ML should plan early because prerequisites and seat availability can shape what is
realistic.

Do not assume the major alone guarantees a deep ML portfolio. Combine the required DSC
sequence with projects, research, internships, or electives in databases, systems,
visualization, ML, or domain applications depending on what kind of data science work you
want to do.
