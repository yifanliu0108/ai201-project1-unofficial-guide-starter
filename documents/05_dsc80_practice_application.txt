=== DSC 80 - Practice/Application Checkpoint Notes ===

Why I saved this: DSC 80 is one of the classes I would plan around, not squeeze in as
an afterthought. It is the bridge from lower-division setup into upper-division work.

Official/course-site idea: DSC 80 is practice and application. It pulls together the
data science lifecycle, algorithms, statistics, visualization, machine learning ideas,
and data systems at a practical level.

Student thread note: people talk about DSC 80 like the continuation of DSC 30, but with
more data analysis in Python. That means dataframe operations, debugging, and clear
written explanations all matter.

My takeaway: review pandas/dataframe work before the quarter starts. DSC 80 matters past
the grade because later DSC classes assume I can clean data, work in notebooks, reason
about messy datasets, and communicate results.
