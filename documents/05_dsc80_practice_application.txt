=== DSC 80 - Practice and Application of Data Science ===

DSC 80 is a major checkpoint course. The public course site frames it as practice and
application of data science, and students often describe it as the class where earlier
Python, pandas, cleaning, visualization, and reasoning skills come together.

Unofficial advice: expect projects and assignments to take real time. Older r/UCSD
threads warned that DSC 80 can feel like the continuation of DSC 30, except with more
data analysis in Python. Students who are shaky on dataframe operations, debugging, or
writing clear explanations should review before the quarter starts.

The course matters beyond its own grade because many later DSC upper divisions assume
you can clean data, work in notebooks, reason about messy datasets, and communicate
results. If you want one course to be organized and well-supported in your schedule, make
it DSC 80.
