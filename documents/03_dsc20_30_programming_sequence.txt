=== DSC 20 and DSC 30 - Programming and Data Structures for Data Science ===

DSC 20 and DSC 30 are where the major starts feeling technical. DSC 20 focuses on
programming fundamentals for data science, while DSC 30 moves into data structures and
algorithms. Student advice consistently says not to overload the quarter you take DSC 30
unless you already have strong programming habits.

The main difficulty is not one huge exam; it is the weekly rhythm of programming work.
Students who start assignments early can debug gradually. Students who wait until the
weekend often lose time to small bugs, hidden cases, or misunderstanding the required
algorithm.

Unofficial planning advice: take DSC 20 seriously even if you already know some Python,
because DSC 30 expects you to reason about code structure, runtime, recursion, and data
structures. If DSC 30 feels hard, that is normal; many UCSD DSC students describe it as
one of the first courses that exposes weak debugging and algorithm habits.
