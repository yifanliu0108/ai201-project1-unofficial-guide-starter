=== DSC 20 and DSC 30 - Programming Sequence Notes ===

Why I saved this: a lot of DSC planning advice comes back to whether someone is ready
for DSC 30. It is one of the first places weak programming habits show up.

DSC 20 is the programming foundation. DSC 30 is where data structures, algorithms,
runtime, recursion, and cleaner code structure become more important. Even if someone
already knows some Python, DSC 30 expects more than getting a notebook cell to run.

Student advice I would actually use: do not overload the DSC 30 quarter unless coding is
already comfortable. Start assignments when they come out, because waiting until the
weekend turns normal debugging into panic debugging. Hidden cases and runtime issues are
easier to fix when there is time to step away and come back.

My takeaway: if DSC 30 feels hard, that is not a sign that I picked the wrong major. It
usually means I need better debugging habits and more practice tracing code.
