# Breville Bambino / Bambino Plus Setup and Recipe

*Source: r/espresso Bambino megathread + community notes (paraphrased)*

The Breville Bambino and Bambino Plus are popular first machines because they heat up in a few seconds (ThermoJet heater) and are compact. They ship with **pressurized (dual-wall) baskets** and **non-pressurized (single-wall) baskets**.

**Recommended recipe:** Use the **single-wall 54mm basket** with a good grinder and pull **18 grams in to 36 grams out (1:2 ratio) in about 25–30 seconds**. The Bambino's default single-shot and double-shot buttons are volumetric (preset volume), but most people switch to pulling manually and stopping by weight on a scale.

The stock pressurized basket masks a bad grind — it forces fake crema regardless of grind quality. It's fine for pre-ground supermarket coffee, but to actually dial in you want the non-pressurized basket plus a burr grinder. The Bambino does **not** include a grinder, and pairing it with a quality burr grinder matters more than anything.

Common Bambino tips from the community:

- The 54mm portafilter takes a 54mm tamper — the included plastic tamper is poor; upgrade to a real 54mm tamper.
- The Bambino Plus has automatic milk steaming with a temperature/foam setting; the base Bambino has a manual steam wand.
- Pre-infusion is built in (low pressure soak before full pressure), which helps puck saturation.
- If shots gush, your grind is too coarse for the single-wall basket — grind finer.

Group head temperature is managed automatically by the ThermoJet, so unlike the Gaggia Classic you do not need to temperature surf.
