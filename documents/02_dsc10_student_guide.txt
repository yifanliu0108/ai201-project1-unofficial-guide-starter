=== DSC 10 - My Starting-Class Notes ===

Why I saved this: DSC 10 is the first DSC class many students use to decide whether the
major fits them. It is also the class people underestimate because it starts friendly.

Official course-listing idea: DSC 10 introduces data exploration, statistical inference,
prediction, Python for tabular data, visualization, simulation, homework, and projects
with real-world datasets.

Student-note version: do not treat labs as copy/paste notebook chores. The useful habits
are reading error messages, checking dataframe shapes, naming variables clearly, and
writing one or two sentences about what a transformation changed. Those habits matter
again in DSC 80.

My takeaway: DSC 10 is not the hardest class in the major, but it is where the notebook
workflow starts. If I leave DSC 10 shaky on Jupyter and tabular data, DSC 20/30/80 will
feel worse than they need to.
