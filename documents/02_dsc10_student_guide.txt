=== DSC 10 - Principles of Data Science ===

DSC 10 is the on-ramp for UCSD data science students. Students describe it as the class
where you learn the workflow of asking a question, cleaning data, using Python notebooks,
making basic visualizations, and explaining results in words. It is friendlier than the
later programming sequence, but it still rewards steady practice.

Unofficial advice: treat labs and projects as skill-building, not busywork. Students who
only copy notebook patterns can pass early assignments but struggle once prompts become
less guided. Build the habit of reading error messages, checking dataframe shapes, and
writing short explanations of what each transformation does.

For a data science major, DSC 10 is most useful if you leave with comfort in Jupyter,
pandas-style tabular thinking, and the idea that analysis is a process. It is not the
hardest course in the major; it is the setup for DSC 20, DSC 30, and DSC 80.
