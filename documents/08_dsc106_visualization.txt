=== DSC 106 - Visualization Notes ===

Why I saved this: visualization is one of the DSC classes that can turn into portfolio
evidence if I save the work instead of treating it as a one-quarter assignment.

Course note: DSC 106 is about communicating analysis. The hard part is not just making a
chart; it is choosing an encoding, making labels readable, and explaining what the viewer
should take away.

Student advice: save strong projects. A clean visualization project is easier to show in
an internship portfolio than a messy notebook with no explanation.

My takeaway: DSC 106 can pair well with a more technical class because the work feels
different from algorithms/systems, but project deadlines can still take time.
