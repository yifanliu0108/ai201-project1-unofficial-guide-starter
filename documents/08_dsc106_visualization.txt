=== DSC 106 - Introduction to Data Visualization ===

DSC 106 is the visualization course. Students usually value it because it connects
technical analysis to communication: choosing encodings, designing charts, and explaining
what a viewer should learn from a dataset.

Unofficial advice: the course is not just "make a pretty graph." Strong work usually
requires a clear question, appropriate chart type, readable labels, and a justification
for design choices. Students who enjoy portfolios should save their best projects
because visualization work can become a useful artifact for internships.

Planning note: DSC 106 pairs well with a more technical class because the workload feels
different from algorithm or systems work. It can still take time near project deadlines,
so do not treat it as a no-work elective.
